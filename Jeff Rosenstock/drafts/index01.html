About Jeff

  • Jeff Rosenstock was born in Long Island, New York, in 1982. He is an American musician, songwriter, producer, and multi-instrumentalist from Long Island, New York.
  • He founded his first band as a young teenager, The Arrogant Sons of Bitches (ASOB) in 1995.
  • He started really getting attention for his second band, Bomb the Music Industry!, a DIY punk/ska project that grew out of ASOB breaking up.
  • Bomb the Music Industry! became notable for its DIY approach and for making music freely available online, with donations encouraged.
  • Rosenstock founded Quote Unquote Records, the first donation-based record label.
  • After Bomb the Music Industry! ended, he began his solo career in 2012 with I Look Like Shit.

Music

Built to Fail (1998)

The Arrogant Sons of Bitches

This is not a good entry point for a new Jeff listener. This is for Jeff completionists unless you're already a fan of low budget ska punk. He recorded this recorded at just 15 years old after 2 years of playing Green Day covers with his friends under the name The Arrogant Sons of Bitches. I enjoy listening to this record even though I don't go back to it hardly ever. But I went back to the record for this blurb and came up with a top 4 tracks based mostly on vibes. It was interesting to hear what Jeff sound like as a teenager. You can hear many stylistic choices he still uses in some of his newer songs but unpolished. It's not for every one but I would actually rate this as a pretty good record. I see the vision.

Top Tracks

  • The Guy From Subway
  • I Hate Punk Rock
  • Abnormality
  • The Song The Girl Sings

Three Cheers for Disappointment ()

The Arrogant Sons of Bitches (2006)

This is the peak of the ASOB experiment. It's fast, loud, and sarcastic. It might be the loudest Jeff Rosenstock record. I return to this record a lot. Jeff was not in his teens when he recorded this record. Most of the band took a break to go to college and the record took 4 years to make. He was 23 when he released this record and his decade of progress comes through in the recording, vocals, and songwriting. It's honestly hard to pick a top 3 tracks because I usually spin the whole record. It flows really well and I'm impressed by most of the tracks on the album. Since it was too hard to stand by three songs being the best I just picked three that I thought covered a good range of vibes the record offers. If you love loud horns and fast drums this would be a great first Jeff record. The recording is even pretty good; which is rare for early Jeff records. I would normally give people We Cool? or Vacation as first records but Three Cheers for Disappointment is a totally valid starting point for a new Jeff fan. Fun fact: the stress of recording this record is allegedly what led to the band breaking up.

Top Tracks

  • The Last Bell I Will Ever Heard/All I Do is Deal With Assholes
  • RocketRocketRocketship
  • Last On My List

Album Minus Band (2005)

Bomb the Music Industry!

I put this record slightly out of chronological order, it came out before Three Cheers for Disappointment, because it's the start of the Bomb the Music Industry! catalogue and that order made more sense to me. This is one of Jeff's more poorly recorded but raw records. Some of the tracks are bad but there's a few gems on the record. My clear favorite and the track that brings me back to the record is actually a Harvey Danger cover, Pike St. Park Slope. This is an example of a ska cover elevating the original on par with Goldinger's 99 Red Balloons or Smash Mouth's Why Can't We Be Friends. The last half of the record is certainly better than the first half and the first track is a hard skip for me. But the last track of the record, Future 86 is probably the best and most popular track on the record. It's hard to draw where the line is, but the record suddenly takes a turn toward better songwriting and arrangment in the second half of the record. I picked the 5 best songs. I'd only listen to the rest of the album if you're a Jeff completionist. There's much more approachable Jeff records ahead.

Top Tracks

  • Future 86
  • Pike St. Park Slope
  • Ready... Set... No!!!
  • Sweet Home Canada
  • FRRRREEEEE BIIIIIIIRRRRD!!!!!!!!! FRRRRREEEEEEEEE BIIIIIIIIIIRRRRRDDD!!!!

To Leave or Die in Long Island (2005)

Bomb the Music Industry!

Under rated but not the most impressive BTMI record by far. The production on this album is better than Album Minus Band but there's several moments where some synths are harsh on the ears. There's also moments where the synths are banging. Jeff was still mastering home production and recording in peoples basements at the time. The lack of production shows but it's still a fun record. With only 8 tracks it doesn't over stay it's welcome even if you feel like there are a few duds. I really enjoy the top 3 I highlighted. But it's less approachable than later BTMI records overall. Syke! Life Is Awesome! is definitely the standout track of the record but the rest of the top three are fun too.

Top Tracks

  • Happy Anterrabae Day!!!
  • Stand There Until You're Sober
  • Syke! Life Is Awesome!

Goodbye Cool World! (2006)

Bomb the Music Industry!

This record is where I would say that BTMI started hitting it's stride. The synths are in good balance for the mix for once. You can actually understand the lyrics in the loud songs. Great ska breakdowns, loud, fast, funny, and sometimes depressing. Everything that makes BTMI great is here, just unpolished. I come back to this record everytime I listen to more than a few Jeff records at a time. I would still recommend a different Jeff record for newbies but this is a still a strong record in Jeff's catalogue. I'm picking the poppiest hits for my top 3 this time because those happen to be the ones I vibe with the most on this record. There were a lot of contenders but these three are the ones I think about first when I come back to the record.

Top Tracks

  • Evening Winning Feels Bad
  • Side Projects are Never Successful
  • Sorry, Brooklyn, Dancing Won't Solve Anything

Get Warmer (2007)

Bomb the Music Industry!

Song about moving, touring, and working dead end jobs. This is a short fun record with a few songs I come back for all the time. The record production is consistent but not as good as later records. Jeff's basement recordings are really reaching listenable mixes by this point in his career. Some of the songs, like the title track, are kind of whiny but the record has plenty of bombastic highs to enjoy. It flows well. I'd definitely recommend it if you like Vacation or Scrambles but it just doesn't stand quite as high as it's peers in the BTMI catalogue.

Top Tracks

  • No Rest for the Whiny
  • Unlimited Breadsticks, Soup and Salad Days
  • Pizza Claus Is Comin' to Town

Scrambles (2009)

Bomb the Music Industry!

This was in my top 3 Jeff records for a long time and may still be. This is the first or second BTMI record I would give to a new fan. I might do a top 5 instead of a top 3 for this one. There's not really any duds on this album and it really connects to people who have been broke in their 20s or you're into the DIY punk scene. I come back to this record sometimes for single listens and it's always one of the first ones I spin on a Jeff listening day. This would be a solid first record to get into as a new Jeff fan. It's got a full synthesis of everything you can expect from a BTMI record and the production quality is pretty good. Highly recommended.

Top Tracks

  • Stuff That I Like
  • It Shits!!!
  • 25!
  • Wednesday Night Drinkball
  • Sort of Like Being Pumped

Adults!!!: Smart!!! Shithammered!!! and Excited by Nothing!!!!!!! (2010)

Bomb the Music Industry!

This isn't usually on my list of new fan recommendations but it's only 20 minutes long and mostly accessible bangers. The low commitment and high banger rate actually makes it a perfect place to get your toes wet. If you like this record check out Vacation next. It's a fun, short record with lots of high highs to enjoy. Highly recommended Jeff material.

Top Tracks

  • All Ages Shows
  • The First Time I Met Sanawon

Vacation (2011)

Bomb the Music Industry!

This one is widely considered by fans to be the best BTMI record. I'm still partial to Scrambles but I love this record a lot. Easily a top 5 Jeff record. One of the best chiptune synth solos ever is in the closing, two part track. It's vulerable, loud, and clever. I easily could have expanded the top tracks list to 5 or more. This is a seminal album for good reason. It's also the last BTMI record. The band broke up after it's release and Jeff began his solo career with I Look Like Shit right after this. I didn't include it but most people would include The Shit That You Hate as a funny, special track when discussing this album. But I find several tracks more relistenable. Since I'm shoving songs in the summary I prefer the song right after The Shit That You Hate, Hurricane Waves.

Top Tracks

  • Campaign For A Better Weekend
  • Everybody That You Love
  • Sunny Place/Shady People and Felt Just Like Vacation

I Look Like Shit (2012)

Jeff Rosenstock

This was Jeff's first solo record and a bombastic start to a long to a long career. It didn't jump out at me at first but it's slowly become on of my favorite Jeff records. It's made of left over songs and covers that never made other albums. So it doesn't tell a cohesive story but it's still full of some of the songs I connect with the most like The Trash The Trash The Trash. My top tracks are really the highlights of the whole record but I find the full thing listenable in one sitting, like most Jeff records. Snow Charges and 80s Through the 50's are some of Jeff's best story telling songs. This record is also one of his least ska influenced records, if the ska was keeping your from listening to Jeff Rosenstock.

Top Tracks

  • The Trash The Trash The Trash
  • Twinkle
  • Snow Charges
  • 80's Through the 50's

We Cool? (2015)

Jeff Rosenstock

This was my first record and I was an instant fan. I felt like Jeff was talking to me dirctly about being 23 and struggling with life and alcohol. I saw The Needledrop give it a 7/10 but described it in a way that made me feel like I would rate it stronger. As soon as I heard Get Old Forever and You, In Wierd Cities I knew I was going to be a life long fan. Jeff is always loud, funny, and sad but this record really showed a sharp evolution in his songwriting. This was his first studio record and it attracted a lot of attention for him. We Cool? really launched Jeff's career and is a great first Jeff record if you were looking for a place to get into the artist. Highly recommended.

Top Tracks

  • Get Old Forever
  • You, In Weird Cities
  • Nausea

Worry. (2015)

Jeff Rosenstock

This is widely considered Jeff's best record and I think it deserves that accolade. This is the record my favorite quote comes from, "Love is worry". It's simple, genius, and heart aching. Some of Jeff's sharpest writing is on this record. It was the first of his records to be reviewed by Pitchfork and it scored an 8/10. It's a top 3 Jeff record even if I don't go back to it as much as the rougher material. Highly recommended, great first Jeff record.

Top Tracks

  • Festival Song
  • Wave Goodnight To Me
  • ...While You're Alive

Post (2018)

Jeff Rosenstock

This is my least favorite Jeff record even though it has the highest Pitchfork score. Some of these songs genuinely ask the audience for a lot of patience. But there's a few highlights. This record actually has my second favorite Jeff quote, "...every cigarette you smoke because your addicted to a quiet source of company...". That hits hard as a smoker. I don't smoke cigarettes but I love cigars, I have a wretched vape pen, and I have a medical card. A quiet source of company were words I never thought of but it describes smoking even more perfectly than just saying, "it makes you look cool". I would skip this album but I would insist on listening to 9/10. This one is mostly a skip for me though. I never come back to this record. I'm only doing a top 2. It's still a good record but it's low teir Jeff for me.

Top Tracks

  • 9/10
  • Yr Throat

No Dream (2020)

Jeff Rosenstock

For two records in a row Jeff wasn't as loud as I'm used to. No Dream is a return to form. Much louder than the previous two albums but still emotionally frustrated. Not as sad as usually, mostly frustrated with the systems we live under. This is one of my favorite Jeff records and a perfect place for a new Jeff fan to jump in. This 3rd studio album released to much acclaim from fans and critics. This is a top teir Jeff rocord and I'll probably include more than a top 3. This record is accessible and can appeal to a broader audience than just Jeff fans. Highly recommended.

Top Tracks

  • Scram!
  • Monday At The Beach
  • Honeymoon Ashtray
  • Ohio Tpke
  • Old Crap

Ska Dream (2021)

Jeff Rosenstock

This is a unique record. It's the same record as No Dream but all ska covers of each song. It provides a totally different vibe. Some songs are straight forward covers of the original but many songs are genuine rewrites that are interesting takes on the original material. If you like No Dream and like ska you will love Ska Dream. Fans argue about which record is the best. I go back to the original more often but I still spin Ska Dream all the time. It's really hard for me to compare the two. This record is just as strong as the original. So, highly recommended.

Top Tracks

  • SKrAm!
  • S K A D R E A M
  • Ohio Porkpie

HELLMODE (2023)

Jeff Rosenstock

This is the newest Jeff Rosenstock record. I haven't listened to it enough to have strong opinions yet. But it's some of Jeff's softer more emotionally mature music. This is where Jeff is now. I think a new Jeff fan should start in the middle of his career and check out HELLMODE if they're still curious after Worry. This record isn't as loud as the others but still fun with driving drums and bass. It's not low teir Jeff by any means it's just not an instant classic like We Cool?. Still recommended but not essential Jeff listening. If you only do one track from this record, I'd recommend HEALMODE.

Top Tracks

  • LIKED U BETTER
  • I WANNA BE WRONG
  • HEALMODE
